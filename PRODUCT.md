# T-Secure

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-approved Vite, HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Firebase Auth and Firestore, Nemotron, Vercel.

## Users and purpose
Seniors need understandable scam warnings and an easy way to pause and independently verify. Before/during/after protection, rather than a promise to identify all fraud.

## Confirmed constraints
Live call protection captures microphone audio with permission, transcribes actual audio through ElevenLabs, and checks every 1–2 sentences with Nemotron. Practice uses fictional, interactive Nemotron caller replies voiced by ElevenLabs and the user's spoken responses. Historical conversations remain synthetic demonstrations. Pasted text receives real rules and, when configured, Nemotron analysis. Historical analysis is opt-in and produces an explained demo index. The T-Life view is a concept sharing the same private profile, not an integration with the real app. No automatic blocking or caregiver dashboard.

## Brand commitments
T-Secure, T-Mobile-inspired magenta, calm and reassuring language. No invented carrier access or protection statistics.

## Accessibility
20px body text, 48px minimum action targets, keyboard and screen-reader support, reduced motion, readable mobile and zoom layouts.
