I compared their departure times and counted the stops along each route. Then I checked the walking distance from the final stop to the building and wrote both sets of directions on a page in my notebook.

[neutral] [matter-of-fact] Around noon, I prepared a sandwich and poured a glass of water. I put the plate on the table and moved the notebook to the other side. After eating, I washed the plate, dried it with a cloth, and returned it to the cupboard. I wiped the counter, folded the cloth, and checked the time before putting on my shoes to go to the store.

[excited] [engaged] [interested] At the store, I compared the information printed on three bags of rice. Each bag listed its weight, cooking time, and the amount of water needed for each serving. I calculated how many servings each bag contained and checked the prices on the shelf. Then I looked at the package dimensions and considered where each one would fit in the kitchen cupboard beside the flour.

[excited] [engaged] [interested] On the way home, I stopped beside a noticeboard near the community center. It displayed a calendar with activities arranged in columns for each day of the week. Some sessions lasted thirty minutes, and others lasted an hour. I checked which rooms were listed for the afternoon sessions, then compared the starting times with the opening hours printed on a separate notice beside the calendar.

[neutral] [matter-of-fact] When I arrived home, I placed the shopping bag on the counter and took out the contents. I put the rice in the cupboard and the vegetables in the refrigerator. Then I folded the bag and stored it with the others under the sink. I took off my shoes, placed them beside the door, and hung my jacket on the hook above the shoe rack.

[excited] [engaged] [interested] In the afternoon, I filled three containers with water and set them on the table. The containers had different shapes, but each held the same amount. I measured the height of the water in each one and recorded the results. Then I poured the water into a measuring jug, one container at a time, to compare the volume with the markings along the side of the jug.

[neutral] [matter-of-fact] Before dinner, I returned the containers to the cupboard and dried the table. I opened my notebook to the page with tomorrow’s schedule and added the time of a delivery.
